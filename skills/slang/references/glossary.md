# Glossary

Grouped by **communicative function** — by what you're trying to say, not alphabetically. That's how you actually retrieve a word mid-sentence. Alphabetical lists are for decoding; functional lists are for speaking.

## Register flags

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[SAFE]` | Fine for a casual work Slack in a normal-vibes company. Low risk. |
| `[CASUAL]` | Friends, group chats, social media. Not for work or clients. |
| `[RISKY]` | Vulgar, insulting, or carries an edge you may not intend. Know exactly what it means before it leaves your mouth. |
| `[DECODE ONLY]` | Understand it. Do not say it. Usually Gen Alpha, or an insult that isn't yours to throw. |
| `[DEAD]` | Actively marks you as out of touch. Listed so you can *stop* saying it. |

`(AAVE)` = African American Vernacular English. `(ballroom)` = Black queer ballroom/drag culture. These aren't trivia; see the usage guide on why the origin governs the register.

| **W** | a win | "big W for the team" / "took the W" | `[SAFE]` |
| **based** | admirably true to yourself, unbothered by opinion | "based take" | `[CASUAL]` |
| **valid** | legitimate, defensible | "honestly valid" | `[SAFE]` |

## 9. Gen Alpha layer — `[DECODE ONLY]`

You will hear all of these. **Do not say any of them.** They belong to people aged 8–14. Adult usage is the canonical cringe failure, and it is the thing that kills the term.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **6-7** ("six seven") | Dictionary.com's 2025 Word of the Year and it means **nothing**. An interjection, often with both palms up alternating. From Skrilla's "Doot Doot (6 7)," spread via LaMelo Ball (6'7") highlights. Its meaninglessness is the point — it's a membership signal, not a word. |
| **skibidi** | From the "Skibidi Toilet" web series. No stable meaning; can be good, bad, or filler. The flagship brainrot word. |
| **gyatt** | Large buttocks; from a drawn-out "god damn." `[RISKY]` and juvenile. |
| **fanum tax** | Taking a bite of a friend's food. Named for streamer Fanum. |
| **sussy** / **sussy baka** | Suspicious. From *Among Us* + Japanese "baka" (fool). Already stale. |
| **only in Ohio** | Tag for anything surreal or deranged. |
| **rizzler** | One who has rizz. The `-er` form is the tell that it's aged into Alpha. |
| **mewing** | Pressing your tongue to your palate to sharpen your jawline. Pseudoscience. |
| **sigma** | Lone-wolf archetype; now almost purely ironic. |
| **aura farming** | Deliberately accumulating cool. |
| **41** | Successor nonsense-number to 6-7, even more opaque. |

**The lifecycle, which is the real lesson:** a term starts in a niche → spreads on TikTok → adults and brands adopt it → it dies. By the time a term reaches a listicle, a marketing deck, or this file, its clock is running. Assume anything here is older than it feels.

## 10. Dead — stop saying these

These actively date you. Most are Millennial, not Gen Z, and several were never Gen Z at all.

**Hard dead:** yeet · on fleek · bae · adulting · totes · "I can't even" · YOLO · "living my best life" · squad goals · epic fail · "that's so random" · doggo/pupper/floofer · "all the feels" · Netflix and chill · cheugy (ironically, itself now cheugy) · "sending thoughts and prayers" as a joke

**Aging fast:** sus · no cap (still understood, sounds try-hard from adults) · slaps · vibing · bussin' · rizz (mainstream enough that it's now a dad word)

**Millennial tells that aren't slang at all but out you instantly:** the 😂 emoji · "Hahaha" with a capital H · full punctuation on a joke · double space after a period · ending a casual text with a period · "lol" as sincere laughter rather than tone-softener · excessive exclamation points

## 11. Emoji semantics — the fastest-moving tell

| Emoji | Means | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 💀 | that's hilarious | The default laugh. "I'm dead." |
| 😭 | that's hilarious *or* genuinely moving | Context decides. Extremely common. |
| 🥀 | wilted, defeated, ironic despair | Often paired: `💔🥀` |
| 😂 | **nothing — it marks you as over 35** | The single loudest tell. Avoid entirely. |
| 🫩 | exhausted, done | Newer. |
| 🙏 | please / thank you | Not prayer. |
| 💯 | agreement, emphasis | Safe, slightly dated. |
| 🔥 | excellent | Safe, universal. |
| ✨ | ironic sparkle around a phrase | "we're having a ✨normal✨ day" |
| 🤡 | I was a fool | Self-directed. |
| 👀 | noticed, intrigued, or "look at this" | Safe. |
| 🗿 | stoic, unbothered, deadpan | Moai. |
| ❤️ (red) | slightly formal/sincere from young people | 🖤🤍💜 read as more casual. |
| 👍 | **passive-aggressive dismissal** | Reads as "conversation over." Genuinely risky at work. |
| 🙂 | thinly veiled displeasure | Never neutral. |

The 😂 → 💀 shift and the 👍 problem are the two highest-value items on this page. Fix those first; they cost nothing and they're doing the most damage.
